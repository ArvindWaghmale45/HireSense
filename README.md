# HireSense

## AI-Powered Interview & Placement Intelligence Platform

> Prepare Smarter. Interview Better. Get Hired.

HireSense is an AI-powered platform designed to help
students and job seekers prepare for interviews,
aptitude tests, and placement opportunities.

## Core Features

- AI Mock Interviews
- AI Answer Evaluation
- Performance Reports & Analytics
- Aptitude Practice
- Placement Guide
- Career Resources
- Webcam & Microphone Support
